Instructions
  1. Click on the button "Select host plant" to display plant.
  2. Click on the button "Choose experiment motive".
  3. Click on the button "Selection of genes for heterologous expression"
  4. Click on the button "Choose suitable plant expression vectors for the expression of target genes".
  5. Click on the button "Ligate the target genes in the plant expression vector".
  6. Click on the button "Transform explant/plant callus with genetic construct via biolistics (gene gun)".
  7. Click on the button "Select and regenerate plant from transformed plant callus/explant".
  8. Click on the button "Isolate genomic DNA from transformed plant".
  9. Click on the button "PCR using gene specific primers from transformed plant genomic DNA".
  10. Click on the button "Transfer the confirmed transgenic plants to soil".
  11. Click on the button "Extract PHBs from confirmed transgenic plants grown in soil.".
  12. CLick on the button "Quantify the PHBs by GC-MS".
